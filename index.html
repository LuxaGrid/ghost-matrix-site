The problem

Public Wi-Fi is a room full of strangers.

Hotel, cafe and airport Wi-Fi puts your laptop, phone and tablet on the same network as everyone else. Some of those networks are fakes set up to look real. And your TV stick, game console and e-reader can't run a security app at all.

Why Ghost Matrix

Every device protected.
Nothing to install.

Ghost Matrix joins the Wi-Fi you're on and gives your devices their own private, filtered Wi-Fi. Your devices connect to it, and it does the protecting.

Protects what apps can't

Laptops, tablets, TV sticks, game consoles, e-readers. If it joins Wi-Fi, it's covered, with no app on any of them.

Set up in about 2 minutes

A guided setup on its own screen. No admin page, no account, no router settings. Each step waits until it really works.

Spots fake hotspots

If a Wi-Fi starts impersonating the router or drops its security, Ghost Matrix pauses your internet and asks before anything else goes out.

Your lookups stay private

Every website lookup is encrypted to Quad9, which also blocks known malware and phishing sites. The cafe's Wi-Fi can't read where you're going.

Invisible on shared Wi-Fi

Your devices sit behind Ghost Matrix's own firewall, so others on the hotel network can't see or reach them. It even uses its own address on every network.

Fails closed, never open

The firewall tests itself at every start. If protection can't be verified, Ghost Matrix shuts its Wi-Fi instead of letting traffic through unprotected.

No cloud. No account.

Nothing to sign up for, and no dashboard in someone else's cloud. It talks to four named services only, and we tell you which ones.

Updates you can trust

New versions arrive over Wi-Fi, signed by Luxa Grid and checked before they install. If one ever fails its checks, it rolls back by itself.

See it live

Its own screen shows protection, devices, speeds and warnings as they happen. No app to open, no admin page to log in to.

Straight talk

We'd rather tell you now than have you find out later.

  • Ghost Matrix is not a VPN and does not make you anonymous. It filters, firewalls and hides your devices; it doesn't tunnel your traffic.
  • It uses 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, which works almost everywhere but isn't the fastest band.
  • We don't claim features until they're built and tested. When something new arrives, it comes as a signed update.
