1. What Ghost Matrix does

Ghost Matrix joins a Wi-Fi network you choose and provides its own protected Wi-Fi for your devices. It filters ads, trackers and known malicious sites, separates your devices from the network it joins, and can warn you about some signs of a fake or tampered Wi-Fi network.

It is not a VPN. It does not hide your internet address from the websites and services you use, and it does not make you anonymous. No filter catches everything: Ghost Matrix reduces risk, it does not remove it. Keep your devices and apps updated and use your own judgement online.

5. Privacy

Ghost Matrix has no account and does not send your browsing, your devices' traffic or personal information to Luxa Grid. To work, it contacts these third-party services, which see the internet address of the network it is on:

  • Quad9 (9.9.9.9): looks up website names, encrypted when the network allows it, and supplies the malware block list. Quad9's own privacy policy applies.
  • Google connectivity check (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com): detects hotel and airport sign-in pages.
  • pool.ntp.org: sets the clock.
  • GitHub (github.com): checks for and downloads updates. No device ID is sent.

Settings such as Wi-Fi names, passwords and watch preferences are stored on the device only. Holding the BOOT button at power-on forgets the saved home Wi-Fi.
